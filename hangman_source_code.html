How to play

  1. Pick a category — fruits, animals, programming, whatever you fancy.
  2. Pick a difficulty. Each one has its own 50 levels and its own saved progress.
  3. Start at level 1, or tap continue to carry on where you stopped.
  4. Read the clue. It always describes the hidden answer without giving it away.
  5. Some letters are already filled in when the level opens. They are free: no attempts, no hints, no points lost.
  6. Tap a letter, or type one on a physical keyboard, to guess it.
  7. A correct letter fills every place it appears. A wrong one costs an attempt and draws another piece of the hangman.
  8. Hints are limited and each costs 25 points, so keep them for when you are stuck.
  9. Reveal every letter before your attempts run out. Spaces between words are filled in for you.
  10. Finishing a level unlocks the next one. Completed levels can be replayed without losing progress.
  11. Everything is saved on this device, so you can close the browser and come back.
